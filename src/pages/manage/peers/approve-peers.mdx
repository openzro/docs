# Approve peers
The peer approval feature enhances network security by requiring manual administrator approval before a device can join the openZro network.
This feature is handy when network administrators want to ensure access is restricted only to trusted, corporate-managed devices.

When enabled, devices connect to the management service without network access to other resources.
Administrators then can assess whether the peer is eligible to join the network.

<Note>
    Peer approval is implemented end-to-end in openZro — it is not a managed-cloud-only
    feature. The validator at
    [`management/integrations/integrations/validator.go`](https://github.com/openzro/openzro/blob/main/management/integrations/integrations/validator.go)
    keeps a pending flag on each peer and respects
    `ExtraSettings.IntegratedValidatorGroups` for selective enforcement (so service accounts
    or routing peers can be exempt from approval gating).
</Note>

## Enable peer approval
To enable peer approval, navigate to `Settings` → `Authentication` in your openZro dashboard and turn on **Require peer approval** in the **Peer approval** card, then click **Save Changes**.

<p>
    <img src="/docs-static/img/manage/peers/approve-peers/peer-approval-settings.png" alt="peer-approval-settings" className="imagewrapper-big"/>
</p>

<Note>
    While peer approval is off, pending peers are not gated and get network access. Their pending flag is
    kept, so turning approval back on holds them again until an admin approves them.
</Note>

## Approve peer
A peer waiting for approval shows **Approval pending** in the **Notice** column of
the **Peers** list. To approve it, open its row menu (**⋮**) and choose **Approve**,
or open the peer and click **Approve** next to its name. You need permission to
update peers.

## Combine with Device Admission
Peer approval is a manual check. To check devices automatically, openZro integrates with EDR and MDM providers
(Microsoft Intune, SentinelOne, Huntress, CrowdStrike Falcon) through
[Device Admission](/manage/access-control/posture-checks/device-admission). Device Admission is a separate gate: a
device the provider does not report as compliant is refused at enrollment, login and sync, rather than left pending.
Both gates can be on at the same time; a new device then has to pass the EDR check and wait for an admin. See
[EDR integrations](/manage/access-control/endpoint-detection-and-response) for the provider setup.

## Skip approval for internal groups

Peers in a group listed under **Groups that skip approval** (in **Settings →
Authentication**, below **Require peer approval**) are approved automatically.
Use it to keep approval for third parties only: list your internal staff
groups, routing peers or service accounts, and every other new device waits
for an admin. A peer in any listed group skips the check.

- Only existing groups can be listed, and a group on the list cannot be
  deleted.
- Removing a group sends back to pending only the peers that group had let
  through. Peers that were approved when they registered stay approved.
- Changes are recorded in the activity log.

Through the API, the list is `settings.extra.peer_approval_exempt_groups` on
`PUT /api/accounts/{accountId}`. Omitting the field keeps the current list,
and an empty list clears it.

Peer approval for third parties is also step 1 of switching on
[2FA on device sign-in](/manage/settings/multi-factor-authentication#2-fa-on-device-sign-in),
where it stays on as a backstop.
