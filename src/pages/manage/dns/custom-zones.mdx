# Custom Zones

openZro's Custom Zones define private DNS records that are distributed directly to peers. Unlike nameservers that forward queries to external DNS servers, Custom Zones are hosted within openZro and resolved locally on peers.

This provides complete control over internal DNS without running separate infrastructure, enabling group-based access control where different teams see different records. Custom Zones are ideal for internal service discovery, environment isolation, split-horizon DNS, and providing friendly names to resources across infrastructure.

## Managing Zones

### Creating a Custom Zone

Go to **DNS**, open the **DNS Zones** tab and click **Add Zone**. The **Add DNS Zone** dialog has two tabs.

<img src="/docs-static/img/manage/dns/zones/add-zone.png" alt="Add DNS Zone dialog" className="imagewrapper"/>

On the **Zone** tab:

- **Name**: a human-readable label for the zone, for example `Internal services`.
- **Domain**: the zone apex, for example `corp.internal`. All records in the zone use it as their suffix. It must be a
  valid FQDN, cannot be changed after creation, and must not overlap the openZro peer DNS domain or another zone.
- **Enable Zone**: on by default. When off, the zone and its records are kept but not distributed to peers, which is
  useful for pausing a zone without deleting it.
- **Append to search domain**: off by default. When on, peers can resolve bare names against the zone (`wiki` →
  `wiki.corp.internal`). When off, use full names.

Click **Continue**. On the **Distribution** tab, select one or more **Distribution Groups**: only peers in these groups
receive the zone. At least one group is required. Click **Add Zone**.

<img src="/docs-static/img/manage/dns/zones/zone-created.png" alt="DNS Zones list" className="imagewrapper-big"/>

### Adding Records to a Zone

Click the zone to open its page, then click **Add Record**. Records are edited inline in the **DNS Records** table:

- **Type**: A (IPv4 address), AAAA (IPv6 address) or CNAME (alias to another domain).
- **Name**: the host name inside the zone, for example `wiki` for `wiki.corp.internal`. Use `@` for the zone apex.
- **Content**: the IP address, or the target domain for a CNAME.
- **TTL**: how long, in seconds, resolvers may cache the record. Defaults to 300 (5 minutes) if left empty.

Click the check mark to save the record.

<img src="/docs-static/img/manage/dns/zones/add-record.png" alt="Zone page with records and the inline Add Record row" className="imagewrapper-big"/>

### Updating a Zone

To change a zone's name, distribution groups or toggles, open the row menu (**⋮**) of the zone and choose **Edit**, or
click **Edit zone** on the zone's page. Make the changes and click **Save Changes**. The domain cannot be changed.

### Deleting a Zone

Open the row menu (**⋮**) of the zone, choose **Delete**, and confirm.

<Note>
Deleting a zone also removes all of its records. To remove a single record, delete it from the zone's page instead.
</Note>

<img src="/docs-static/img/manage/dns/zones/delete-zone.png" alt="Delete zone confirmation" className="imagewrapper"/>


## Behavior

### DNS Resolution Precedence

Custom DNS Zones take precedence over nameservers when there is a conflict. If a nameserver is configured with a match domain that is the same as a Custom DNS Zone domain, the zone's records will be resolved first, and the nameserver will not be queried for that domain.

A name inside a distributed zone that has no record gets an authoritative `NXDOMAIN` answer; it is not forwarded to
nameservers.

## Limitations

Custom DNS Zones have the following limitations:

- **Domain cannot be changed**: Once a zone is created, its domain cannot be modified. The zone must be deleted and recreated with a new domain
- **Cannot use peer DNS domain**: Zone domain must not conflict with your openZro peer DNS domain (`openzro.selfhosted` by default, or the domain set with the management server's `--dns-domain` flag or in **Settings** > **Networks** > **Custom DNS domain**)
- **No overlapping zones**: A zone cannot be inside or contain another zone's domain
- **CNAME exclusivity**: CNAME records cannot coexist with A or AAAA records for the same hostname
- **Empty zones not distributed**: Zones without any DNS records are not distributed to peers
- **Record types**: Only A, AAAA and CNAME records are supported; wildcard records are not
