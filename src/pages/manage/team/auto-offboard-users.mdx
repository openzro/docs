# Offboard Team Members

When someone leaves, or a temporary engagement ends, remove their access to openZro and to the devices they enrolled.

## Block, deactivate or delete a user

Go to **Identity → Users & Groups → Users**.

- **Block:** open the user and turn on **Block User**. The user can no longer sign in, and their peers are expired and
  disconnected. Unblock to restore access.
- **Deactivate** (local users): the password is removed, the user is blocked and their peers are expired. This is
  permanent.
- **Delete:** removes the user and their peers.

Blocking keeps the user and their peers visible for audit, so prefer it when you may need to review what the user did.

## Offboard at the identity provider

Disabling the user at your identity provider stops new sign-ins, but a peer that is already logged in stays connected
until its session expires. To make identity provider changes take effect on devices, turn on
**Peer session expiration** under **Settings → Authentication**; peers then have to sign in again periodically, and a
disabled user cannot.

## Offboard over SCIM

When your identity provider or identity governance tool provisions users over [SCIM](/manage/team/idp-sync), it
offboards them too:

- Setting a user inactive, or deleting it, blocks the user in openZro and expires their peers.
- Removing a user from a SCIM group removes them from that group, and the access granted to that group through
  [access control policies](/manage/access-control/manage-network-access) goes with it.

<Warning>
SCIM offboarding currently reaches only [local users](/manage/team/idp-sync/scim-local-users). Users who sign in
through an external identity provider are not linked to their SCIM record yet
([openzro#255](https://github.com/openzro/openzro/issues/255)), so block them in the dashboard or rely on peer session
expiration.
</Warning>
