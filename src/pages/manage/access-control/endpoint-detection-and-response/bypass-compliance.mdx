# Admission Bypass for Non-Compliant Peers

When [Device Admission](/manage/access-control/posture-checks/device-admission) is enforced, a device that fails its
MDM/EDR check is refused at login and sync. An **admission bypass** lets one peer in anyway, for a limited time, for
example a laptop that is being remediated or a device that needs access before its agent reports in.

A bypass:

- applies to one peer;
- requires a reason, which is recorded in the activity log;
- always expires, after at most 30 days. It does not end when the device becomes compliant;
- skips only the Device Admission gate. [Posture checks](/manage/access-control/posture-checks) attached to access
  control policies still apply, so a non-compliant peer may still be left out of those policies.

## Grant a bypass

You need permission to update peers.

1. Go to **Peers** and open the row menu (**⋮**) of the peer.
2. Choose **Bypass admission…**.
3. Enter a **Reason** and pick an **Expiry**: 1 hour, 4 hours, 24 hours (default), 7 days or 30 days.
4. Confirm. The peer can connect on its next login attempt.

To keep a device in for longer, grant a new bypass before the current one expires, or fix the underlying compliance
issue.

## Revoke and list bypasses

Revoking and listing are available through the API:

```bash
# Grant (same as the dashboard action)
curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer <TOKEN>" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"reason": "Intune re-enrollment pending", "expires_in_seconds": 86400}' \
  https://<YOUR_MANAGEMENT_DOMAIN>/api/peers/<PEER_ID>/admission-bypass

# Show the active bypass for a peer
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer <TOKEN>" \
  https://<YOUR_MANAGEMENT_DOMAIN>/api/peers/<PEER_ID>/admission-bypass

# Revoke it
curl -X DELETE -H "Authorization: Bearer <TOKEN>" \
  https://<YOUR_MANAGEMENT_DOMAIN>/api/peers/<PEER_ID>/admission-bypass

# List all active bypasses in the account
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer <TOKEN>" \
  https://<YOUR_MANAGEMENT_DOMAIN>/api/admin/admission-bypasses
```

Instead of `expires_in_seconds`, you can pass `expires_at` as an RFC 3339 timestamp.

## Audit

Every change is recorded in the [activity log](/manage/activity):

- **Peer admission bypass granted**, with the initiator, reason and expiry;
- **Peer admission bypass revoked**;
- **Peer admission bypass expired**, when the expiry worker removes it.

<Note>
    For routing peers and servers that are not enrolled in an MDM/EDR at all, use Device Admission's exempt groups
    instead of repeated bypasses.
</Note>
